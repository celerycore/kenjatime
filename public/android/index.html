Kenjatime 0.4.1

The current Three.js arena, packaged for Android. Your fighters, stages and included sample run offline with Kenjatime’s interface. Matches play in the same view with touch controls and a fixed aspect ratio.

Download Kenjatime for Android Version 0.4.1 · Android 7.0+ · Android System WebView 107 or newer · Signed test APK
  • Import ZIP, RAR4/RAR5, 7z, TAR, TAR.GZ/TGZ, TAR.BZ2/TBZ2, TAR.XZ/TXZ and TAR.ZST/TZST archives.
  • Archives are identified by their contents, including downloads with missing or incorrect file extensions.
  • Save archive keeps an original-byte copy you can save or share again.
  • Adaptive audio buffering helps absorb brief rendering stalls. Audio follows the device’s output sample rate.
  • Reset library remains available during startup. Use it to remove installed copies and restore the included fighter.
  • Kenjatime controls, lifebars, palettes and controller support. No stock IKEMEN menus.

Open the downloaded APK with Android’s package installer. Install over the matching Kenjatime browser-runtime app to keep its installed library. Your browser library and the separate native test app have their own storage. New imports retain their original archive; older installed packages remain playable.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the copies saved inside it. Imports accept up to 128 MB compressed, 512 MB expanded and 5,000 entries. Multi-part volumes need to be combined first. Use the archive password field for supported encrypted archives.
