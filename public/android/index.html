Web engine test app

Import your MUGEN fighters and stages, save them on your device, and play in a landscape arena.

Download Android test APK Version 0.1.4 · Android 7.0+ · Android System WebView 107 or newer
  • Offline arena and included Kung Fu Man sample.
  • ZIP, RAR and 7z imports and a saved archive library.
  • Touch controls and browser-standard controllers, including PS5 mapping.
  • Low graphics by default, with 60 Hz combat simulation.

Open the downloaded APK with Android's package installer. Install over your existing version to keep your app data. Choose PixiJS under Renderer to try the optional WebGL prototype; Canvas remains the default. This is a test build using the current web engine through Android System WebView. MUGEN compatibility remains incomplete, and faster combat is not guaranteed.

Your original character archives remain separate. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the copies saved inside the app.
